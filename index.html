WHAT THE SYSTEM DOES

Seven connected modules that run the whole store operation — from taking an order to reporting on performance.

01

Dashboard

Overview
  • Total sales summary
  • Today's transactions count
  • Total number of products
  • Low-stock products alert
  • Revenue summary for the period
02

Point of Sale

Checkout
  • Product search and categories
  • Add products to cart
  • Increase / decrease quantity
  • Remove cart items
  • Subtotal, tax and total calculation
  • Payment input and change calculation
  • Complete transaction & print receipt
03

Inventory Management

Stock Control
  • Add new products
  • Edit product information
  • Delete products from catalog
  • Product quantity tracking
  • Product pricing
  • Product category assignment
  • Stock status and low-stock notification
04

Sales Management

History
  • Full transaction history
  • Receipt information per sale
  • Date and time of transaction
  • Total amount paid
  • Payment method used
  • Customer information per transaction
05

Customer Management

Buyers
  • Add new customers
  • Customer name record
  • Contact information (phone / email)
  • Purchase history per customer
06

Reports

Analytics
  • Daily sales report
  • Weekly sales report
  • Monthly sales report
  • Total revenue calculation
  • Best-selling products ranking
07

User Management

Access
  • Admin account with full access
  • Cashier account for POS use
  • Secure login interface
  • Different user permissions per role

📈 How It Works

Step 1
User logs in with an Admin or Cashier account.
Step 2
Cashier searches and selects PC parts to build the cart.
Step 3
System computes subtotal, tax, total and change.
Step 4
Transaction is saved, stock updates, receipt is generated.
Step 5
Admin reviews sales, inventory and reports.
